May 2026 · Solo build

Credit Risk & Portfolio Optimization Engine

An end-to-end probability-of-default system on 30,000 credit card accounts. A credit decisioning tool that turns 30,000 account histories into limit recommendations.

  • XGBoost PD model at 0.82 ROC-AUC, with reject inference so the model learns from rejected applicants too.
  • Engineered behavioural, delinquency and utilisation features; statistical feature selection to cut noise.
  • SHAP explainability on every prediction, so a limit change can be justified per account.
  • Segments accounts into three behavioural risk bands and recommends credit limits inside policy guardrails.
  • Built for portfolio operations: every recommendation comes with the reason behind it, not just a score.
  • Shipped as a Streamlit app so a non-technical reviewer can run scenarios themselves.

Feb 2026 – present · Research intern, TIET

Type 2 Diabetes Screening Pipeline

A clinical ML pipeline built on 16 biomarkers, tuned for the errors that matter. A screening tool framed as a patient-safety problem rather than an accuracy contest.

  • Built and compared six models — Random Forest, SVM, Logistic Regression and others — reaching 0.84 ROC-AUC.
  • Recall-focused threshold optimisation cut missed cases by 66%, treating false negatives as the real cost.
  • Findings presented at AITA 2026, an international AI conference.
  • Reframed misclassification as clinical risk: a missed case costs a patient, a false alarm costs a test.
  • Threshold tuning reduced missed cases by 66% — the number a hospital would actually care about.
  • Presented the work at AITA 2026, an international AI conference.

Galaxy Cluster Mass & Dark Matter

A data-driven astrophysics project on real telescope survey data. Astrophysics, but the job was the same: pull signal out of a huge public dataset.

  • Used SDSS spectroscopic data to identify galaxy cluster members and compute velocity dispersion.
  • Estimated the cluster's total dynamical mass and benchmarked it against luminous mass to quantify the dark-matter contribution.
  • Turned raw Sloan Digital Sky Survey data into a mass estimate — a full measurement pipeline end to end.
  • Compared dynamical vs luminous mass to reason about what the data implies about dark matter.
